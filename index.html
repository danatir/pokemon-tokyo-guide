What to stamp where

Checked against your actual book. One press per station — never two.

1
At each station: one press, in that station's own boxEvery station has its own printed page slot with its name on it. Nothing else goes in it. The gate and the hours are printed under the box — trust those over anything on this page.
2
At Ōimachi: staff tick the 6駅コース boxPage 5, small box marked 係員チェック欄. You do not press anything.
3
At Tokyo Station: you press two big boxes yourselfPage 5, 9駅コース and 36駅コース. The achievement stamps sit at that counter — 達成スタンプはゴールカウンターに設置しています.
✕
Forget "press twice" — I had that wrongThat rule is for the free handout book, which only covers the 6-station course and hands you a second, empty book at Ōimachi. Yours carries every course in one, one slot per station. Nothing is ever stamped twice.
One station, one box. 上野駅 — and under it, 【スタンプ設置場所】入谷改札外 【スタンプ設置時間】始発〜終電. Every station page looks like this.
Page 5 — the only other things you stamp. Two big boxes you press at Tokyo Station, two small 係員チェック欄 boxes the staff handle.
Two gates I got wrong Ueno is 入谷改札外, not just any gate. Akabane is 北改札口外 自由通路 — north, not south. The rest of the gate names on this page came from the brochure; your book prints the real one under every box, so if they disagree, the book wins.
Add LINE now @cpn-jrepokemon2026 — Friday's prize at Ōimachi is not handed over without the coupon it sends.
Stamp hours 始発〜終電, first train to last, at nearly every station. Ōfuna stops at 22:00. Counters are the exception: 11:30–18:00.
