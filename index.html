Tickets — short answer: two days only

You are on Apple Pay Suica, so nothing here needs a machine or a window. Tap through on weekdays; buy one pass on Saturday and one on Sunday.

Weekdays — just tapWed, Thu, Fri, Mon
no pass▶
Why
Each weekday run stays on a handful of adjacent stations. A day pass only pays off when you cross a lot of distance, and these days do not.
Friday
The 都区内パス ¥870 would save you roughly ¥200 on Friday morning. Real, but small, and it has to be bought after you are already inside the JR area. Take it or leave it.
Cost
Wednesday about ¥1,800 · Thursday about ¥2,100 · Friday about ¥1,300 · Monday about ¥1,000.
のんびりホリデーSuicaパス — ¥2,850Saturday and Sunday
buy both days▶
Why
Saturday reaches Ōfuna and Machida; Sunday reaches Hasuda and Chiba. Unpassed, each day runs ¥3,300–3,600 in fares. The pass saves roughly ¥500–800 a day and you stop thinking about fares.
How
Wallet → your Suica → チケット購入 → のんびりホリデーSuicaパス → buy. It attaches to the card you already tap with. About a minute, on the platform if you like.
Timing
Same-day only — you cannot buy Saturday's pass on Friday night. Buy it before you tap in at Nippori.
Covers
JR East across Tokyo, Kanagawa, Saitama, Chiba. Normally weekends only, but it also runs every day from 20 July to 31 August, so your whole rally week qualifies.
Does not cover
Tokyo Monorail to Haneda — ¥520 each way on Saturday. Also the Nippori-Toneri Liner, which is Toei. And Ryūgasaki-shi sits just outside the zone.
Outside the zone
Nothing to do. Tap out as normal and the extra fare comes off your Suica balance.
